Marcus Lee
Thank you, Dana, and good morning. I'll walk through the quarter and then our updated outlook.

Revenue of $1.42 billion grew 8.4%. Fuel surcharge revenue was roughly flat year over year as lower diesel prices offset higher volume. Excluding fuel, revenue grew 9.6%.
